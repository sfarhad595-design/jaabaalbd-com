# Reminders — things the owner must do or pay

The daily briefing reads this file every morning (Bangladesh time) and reminds
the owner of anything due today, overdue, or due in the next 3 days.
Format: one line per item. Never delete a line: when done, tick it and add the
date; finished items move to memory/archive/reminders-YYYY.md once a year.

Repeat values: none · monthly on day N · yearly · weekly <day>.
For a fixed date with money attached, also put it on the owner's Google
Calendar (with a phone alert) when they agree.
